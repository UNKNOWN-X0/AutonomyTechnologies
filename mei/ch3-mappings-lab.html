Mappings Lab

Mathematics for Engineers I · Module 62765 · Chapter 3

Predict what the mapping, graph, or point will do. Rate your confidence, reveal the mechanism, then explore new values after the four challenges.

How the lab works

  1. Predict. Commit to an answer before the stage changes.
  2. Rate your confidence. A sure but wrong answer marks an idea to revisit first.
  3. Reveal. Follow the inputs through the rule and compare the result with your prediction.
  4. Explore. Finish all four challenges to unlock that experiment’s controls.
