How the lab works

Choose a prediction, then say how sure you are. The reveal animates the mechanism and leaves your prediction beside the calculated result. Use Explore freely at any time to change the controls; your place in the challenges is kept.

Each experiment has four fixed challenges. After answering all four, its sandbox stays open and the lab records the skill on the course page.
